Install

The repository is a uv workspace. Cloning it gives you the library, the web app and the sample data.

To use only the library in another project, install it straight from the repository. It needs Python 3.10 or later.

Step 1 Write the question before opening the data.

The steps after this one are mechanical. This one is not, and it decides what the rest will mean. For the sample survey:

QuestionAmong respondents who were exposed to the campaign, how much higher is each brand KPI than it would have been without exposure?
EstimandATT, the effect on the exposed.
ExposureThe group column, Exposed against Control.
How exposure happenedYounger respondents, heavy social and streaming users, and current brand users were more likely to be reached. Device and ad blocking mattered too.
CovariatesDemographics, media habits, prior brand usage and category purchase frequency. All describe the respondent before the campaign.
Left out on purposeThe attitude scales and the brand loyalty score. In a post-campaign survey the ad could have moved them.
Primary KPIAided awareness. The others are secondary.
Placebo checkunaided_awareness, which the simulation gives no true effect. Adjusted lift on it should be near zero.

Step 4 Run it.

The run analysed 1,208 exposed and 1,792 control respondents. Read the warnings before anything else. They are the package telling you where the analysis departs from what you asked for.

Two of these matter. Matching left 123 exposed respondents without a control inside the caliper and its exact-match cells, so its estimate covers the rest. And overlap weights answer for the overlap population, not the exposed.

Step 5 Check overlap before any lift number.

If comparable respondents do not exist, nothing downstream can be trusted. Look at where the two groups sit on the propensity score.

The overlap coefficient of 0.71 is in the workable range, and nearly every exposed respondent sits inside the range the controls cover. The groups differ, visibly, but controls exist across the whole exposed distribution. The analysis can proceed without trimming.

Overlap is also reported feature by feature, which is where you find a level or a range that only one group has.

Weighting leaves nothing above the threshold. Matching leaves one variable just over it and keeps far fewer effective controls, which is why its intervals will be the widest. Overlap weights balance almost exactly, as they are built to.

Step 8 Read the lift.

Now open the outcomes. For each KPI, compare the raw difference with what each method reports. A hollow marker means the interval includes zero.

  • raw difference
  • adjusted, significant
  • adjusted, not significant

What these results say

  • Adjustment shrinks every KPI. Consideration goes from a raw +14.0 pp to +8.0 pp under IPW. The gap between them is the part of the raw difference that the measured covariates explain.
  • The weighting methods agree. IPW, overlap weights, stratification and AIPW land within about a point of each other on most KPIs. That says the result does not depend on how the score is used. It says nothing about whether the covariates are sufficient.
  • The placebo behaves. Unaided awareness shows a raw +3.6 pp that is pure confounding. After adjustment the weighting methods put it at −3.1 pp with intervals that include zero.
  • One false positive. Matching reports the same placebo as significant, p = 0.046. With six KPIs and five methods, one result at that level is expected by chance. It is also the method that dropped 123 exposed respondents.
Why there is no “true effect” line on most KPIs.

The simulation sets its effects on a latent scale (log-odds), which does not convert to one fixed number of percentage points. Unaided awareness is the exception: its true effect is zero on any scale.

Step 9 Break it out by subgroup, carefully.

Columns in subgroup_columns get their own contrasts, using the same weights or matches as the main analysis. A level needs at least 10 exposed and 10 control respondents to be reported.

The small group has an interval several times wider than the others. A subgroup difference is only worth reporting when the intervals clearly separate, and each extra breakout is another chance to find a pattern in noise. Decide which subgroups matter before you run.

Step 10 Report it so a reader can judge it.

A lift number without its assumptions invites the reader to treat it as an experiment. Put these alongside it:

  • The question. Population, exposure definition, estimand.
  • The sample. How many exposed and control, and how many were dropped, trimmed or left unmatched.
  • What was adjusted for, and what you believe drove exposure but could not measure.
  • Overlap and balance. The overlap coefficient, the largest standardised difference after adjustment, the effective number of controls.
  • Every method you ran, not only the one you lead with.
  • The placebo result, if you ran one.

The result object serialises to the same JSON the HTTP API returns, and the CLI runs the whole analysis from a config file.
