Use cases

Recipes for the common situations.

Each recipe gives the config, what to check afterwards, and the catch. They assume df is loaded and a base cfg exists, as in the walkthrough.

Brand lift for one campaign

Situation
One exposed group, one control group, a handful of KPIs. You want the effect on the people the campaign reached.
Check afterwards
The overlap coefficient is above 0.5, no variable is imbalanced after adjustment, and ipw and aipw agree.
The catch
The answer is only as good as the covariate list. Write down what drove exposure before choosing features.

A sample that was quota-matched

Situation
Controls were recruited to mirror the exposed group on age band, gender and region. You want the analysis to respect those cells.
Check afterwards
matching.n_exposed_unmatched. Exact cells plus a caliper can leave many exposed respondents without a match.
The catch
Quota matching balances the quota variables and nothing else. Media habits and brand history still need to be in model_features.

Text scales and top-two box

Situation
Purchase intent is a five-point text scale and the business reads it as “definitely or probably will”.
How outcomes are read
Yes/No, True/False and 0/1 columns become proportions. Numeric scales are reported as means. A text scale needs its positive levels listed, and is then recoded to 1 and 0.
The catch
Choose the box before you see results. Top box and top-two box can tell different stories, and picking afterwards is a fork.

Several exposure cells, or messy group codes

Situation
The group column holds more than two values: several creative cells, or codes such as “Unknown”. The example uses a column named cell.
How rows are assigned
Rows matching exposed_values are exposed and rows matching control_values are control. Everything else is dropped and counted. If control_values is left unset, every other non-missing value becomes control, with a warning.
The catch
Each cell's ATT describes that cell's own audience. Two cells can differ because they reached different people, so do not rank creatives on these numbers alone.

Missing answers

Situation
Some respondents skipped income, or a KPI question.
What happens by default
With missing_strategy="impute", numeric features are median-imputed and gain a missing-indicator column, so the two groups are also balanced on who skipped the question. A missing categorical answer is treated as its own level. A respondent missing a KPI is dropped for that KPI only.
The catch
"drop" removes every respondent with any missing feature. That is simpler to explain and can change who the sample represents.

Thin overlap

Situation
The overlap coefficient is low, a few controls carry most of the weight, or the run warns that exposed respondents score above every control.
Options
Trim to the shared score range, switch to overlap weights, or both. Compare the effective number of controls before and after.
The catch
All of these narrow the population. Report how many exposed respondents were removed, and describe the estimate as applying to the comparable ones.

Doubting the model's shape

Situation
Exposure may depend on the covariates in ways a logistic model misses: thresholds, interactions, frequency caps.
Options
Add aipw, which is consistent when either the score model or the outcome model is right. Rerun with gradient_boosting and compare balance.
The catch
Both guard against the wrong functional form. Neither helps with a variable that is missing. With tree models, bootstrap intervals hold the scores fixed and the run says so.

A placebo outcome

Situation
You want evidence about confounding beyond the balance table.
How
Add a KPI the campaign could not have changed: awareness of an unrelated brand, or an attitude the creative never touched. Its adjusted lift should be close to zero.
The catch
A clean placebo is encouraging and proves nothing. A placebo that moves is a clear sign something is unbalanced. In the sample data, unaided_awareness plays this role.

The effect across the whole audience

Situation
The decision is about extending the campaign to everyone, so you want the average effect over exposed and unexposed alike.
Check afterwards
Effective sample size in both groups, and the largest weight. The ATE needs exposed respondents who look like typical controls.
The catch
You are extrapolating the effect to people the campaign did not reach. If those people differ from the reached in how they respond, the data cannot tell you.

Lift by segment

Situation
Stakeholders want lift by gender, age band or region.
How
List the columns in subgroup_columns. Numeric columns with more than 10 values are cut into quintiles. Levels with fewer than 10 respondents in either group are skipped.
The catch
The weights were built to balance the whole sample. Inside a small segment they may not. Read segment results as descriptive, and name the segments you care about in advance.

Does the answer depend on my settings?

Situation
You chose a caliper and a model type, and want to know how much rides on those choices.
How
Rerun over a small, pre-declared grid and report the range. Set n_bootstrap=0 for speed while exploring.
The catch
This is a robustness report for a result you already committed to. Used to pick the setting with the best-looking lift, it is a search for significance.

Many surveys from the command line

Situation
A tracker delivers a new file every wave, with the same columns.
How
Keep one cfg.json under version control and run the CLI per file. dataset_id defaults to the file name.
The catch
A fixed config is the point: the analysis was decided before the data arrived. Keep it fixed, and still read each wave's overlap and warnings.

Over HTTP

Situation
Another system needs to submit analyses and collect results.
How
Upload a file, post an AnalysisConfig that references the returned dataset_id, poll until the job completes, then read the result or download the Excel export. Analyses run in a process pool, so several can run at once.
The catch
The config is validated when you submit it. A column that does not exist, or an empty group, comes back as a 422 with a message.
